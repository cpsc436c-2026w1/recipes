
== 1. A table for each rider's trips in time order: partition key rider_id, sort key requested_at

$ aws dynamodb create-table --table-name recipe-rider-trips --billing-mode PAY_PER_REQUEST --key-schema '[{"AttributeName":"rider_id","KeyType":"HASH"},{"AttributeName":"requested_at","KeyType":"RANGE"}]' --attribute-definitions '[{"AttributeName":"rider_id","AttributeType":"S"},{"AttributeName":"requested_at","AttributeType":"S"}]' --tags Key=purpose,Value=recipe --query TableDescription.TableStatus
"CREATING"

== 2. A table of drivers and their status

$ aws dynamodb create-table --table-name recipe-drivers --billing-mode PAY_PER_REQUEST --key-schema AttributeName=driver_id,KeyType=HASH --attribute-definitions AttributeName=driver_id,AttributeType=S --tags Key=purpose,Value=recipe --query TableDescription.TableStatus
"CREATING"
